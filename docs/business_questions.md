# Business Questions

## Executive
- What are total revenue, profit, orders and active customers?
- How is revenue changing month over month?

## Customers
- What is the repeat-customer rate?
- Who are the highest-value customers?
- Which cities have the highest customer revenue?

## Products
- Which products sell the most units?
- Which products generate the most profit?
- Which high-revenue products have weak margins?

## Stores
- Which stores lead in revenue?
- Which stores lead in profit?
- How does performance differ by city?

## Data Quality
- How many duplicate orders were removed?
- How many missing values were corrected?
- Were there invalid discounts or orphan foreign keys?
